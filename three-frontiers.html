The Hopf fibration

S³ → S², (z₁, z₂) ↦ z₁ / z₂

The 3-sphere, a shape living in four dimensions, can be split into circles so that every point of an ordinary sphere corresponds to exactly one circle. Any two of those circles are linked once, like rings in a chain.

Here we project the circles into 3D. Circles that sit over one latitude of the sphere form a torus, so the whole picture is a set of nested tori.

Reaction and diffusion

∂v/∂t = Dv∇²v + uv² − (F + k)v

In 1952 Alan Turing showed that two chemicals which react while spreading at different speeds can break uniformity on their own. The Gray–Scott version below produces dividing spots, coral and mazes from nothing but two numbers, the feed rate F and kill rate k.

The Kuramoto model

dθᵢ/dt = ωᵢ + K r sin(ψ − θᵢ)

Fireflies, heart cells and power grids all contain thousands of oscillators with slightly different natural rhythms. Below a critical coupling Kc ≈ 1.6 they stay incoherent. Past it, a locked core suddenly appears and the order parameter r grows from zero.

The arrow is the group’s average phase, and its length is r.
